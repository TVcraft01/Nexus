Nexus is the person who is good with electronics, available to anyone. A normal user says what they want
in plain words (set up my Wi-Fi, answer my emails). Nexus works out the device and the steps itself, even for
something it has never seen, does the job, checks that it worked and reports in a sentence. It asks only when
a human or a permission is needed. It learns its user (habits, hours, what they always approve) so over time it
asks less and does more. It works on any device the user owns, wherever they are. Every action has a risk
tier enforced in code. Nexus is an agent, not an app to polish.
